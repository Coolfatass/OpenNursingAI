# Invitation to Join OpenNursingAI

## Building Safer, Human-Reviewed AI for Nursing Practice

Dear colleagues,

OpenNursingAI invites nurses, nurse informaticians, clinical informatics specialists, AI engineers, software developers, researchers, patient-safety professionals, privacy and security experts, ethicists, and healthcare leaders to participate in an open-source collaboration on safer AI for nursing practice.

The project's long-term vision is to explore human-reviewed AI tools that may reduce repetitive nursing work and support workflows such as nursing education, documentation, handoff, task coordination, risk assessment, medication safety, device management, and nursing quality improvement. These are long-term research directions, not current product capabilities.

The current project is a **documentation baseline and research-planning initiative**. There is no runnable MVP at this time. The first focused research direction is a **synthetic-data nursing handoff assistant** that may generate draft SBAR content for review by qualified nursing professionals.

OpenNursingAI is not clinically validated and must not be used for diagnosis, prescribing, medical orders, autonomous clinical decisions, or direct patient care. It is not connected to real HIS, EMR, or hospital operational systems. Qualified nursing professionals must retain final professional judgment. The project does not replace institutional responsibilities or applicable laws and regulations.

We welcome contributors who can help us:

- Identify nursing workflow challenges without sharing patient-identifiable information;
- Review nursing requirements and clinical-safety boundaries;
- Design synthetic nursing cases and evaluation criteria;
- Contribute to privacy, security, ethics, and governance discussions;
- Review documentation, research plans, prototypes, and future Pull Requests;
- Support international and Chinese-language collaboration.

Contributors may begin by joining GitHub Discussions, reviewing the project documentation, proposing synthetic nursing scenarios, or volunteering for nursing, technical, safety, privacy, ethics, or evaluation review. Please describe your background and preferred area of contribution in a Discussion or Issue.

Please do not post patient names, medical records, hospital-internal documents, account credentials, API keys, or other confidential information in the repository. Security vulnerabilities should be submitted through the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel rather than a public Issue or Discussion.
